Lead Live Demo privacy policy

Updated 14 October 2026

Lead Live Demo is a software assignment demonstration operated by the administrator of the Lead Live Demo Facebook Page. Please use fictional test details only. This form is intended to test delivery to a demonstration app.

Information processed

The demonstration receives the name, email address, and any other answers submitted through its test lead form, together with Meta lead, Page and form identifiers and submission timestamps. Facebook may prefill fields with profile information; replace that information with fictional details before testing.

How information is used

Submissions are retrieved from Meta, stored in a local database on the operator's computer, and displayed in the Lead Live app to demonstrate real-time delivery. The operator can view the submitted information. The demonstration does not send marketing messages or sell submissions.

Services and access

Meta processes form submissions under its own privacy policy. If a Cloudflare Tunnel is used to connect the demonstration to the internet, network requests pass through Cloudflare. The demo app requires an access token to view stored leads; anyone given that token can access the demo data. Use only fictional details in recordings or screenshots shared for the assignment.

Storage and deletion

Local submissions remain in the demo database until the operator deletes them; automatic expiry is not implemented. Deleting the local copy does not delete copies held by Meta or information already included in recordings. To request deletion of a local submission, contact the operator through the Lead Live Demo Facebook Page and identify the test submission. Do not send extra sensitive information.

Contact

Send questions about this demonstration or its handling of submissions to the Lead Live Demo Page administrator.
